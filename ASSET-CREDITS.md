# Preview assets

The four photographs currently displayed were supplied by the client as temporary showcase placeholders. They remain labeled as placeholders throughout the site until Major K Media approves the final portfolio.

The transparent `dist/assets/major-k-media-logo.png` was cleaned from the supplied white-background logo for the opening animation and site header.

Bodoni Moda and Manrope are self-hosted in `dist/assets/fonts/`. Their SIL Open Font License files accompany them in the same directory. Source: [Bodoni Moda font files](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bodonimoda) and [Manrope font files](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope).
