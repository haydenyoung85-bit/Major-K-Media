# Photography site research

Researched and scraped on September 10, 2026 with Firecrawl. These are established US photography-site references, not a claim that there is a single definitive national website ranking.

Discovery: [Over The Moon's best wedding photographers](https://blog.overthemoon.com/planning/over-the-moon-best-wedding-photographers/) identifies Jose Villa, KT Merry, and Samm Blake, among others, and links to their official sites.

| Official site reviewed | Observed from the scraped page and branding | Interpretation applied to Major K Media |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Jose Villa](https://josevilla.com/) | Image-led homepage, short navigation, clear contact path, white background, square image treatment. | Keep images prominent and make contacting the photographer straightforward. |
| [KT Merry](https://ktmerry.com/) | Prominent portfolio and inquiry paths, large serif headings, distinct photography categories. | Pair expressive display type with simple navigation and clearly separated collection information. |
| [Samm Blake](https://www.sammblakeweddings.com/) | Extensive photography sequences, compact Portfolio/About/Contact navigation, restrained typography. | Let the photographs set the rhythm, with little interface chrome around them. |

The resulting layout, wording, logo treatment, colors, and code were created for Major K Media. Reference-site copy, logos, photographs, testimonials, awards, and credentials were not reused. Client-supplied preview photographs are visibly marked as placeholders.

## Design direction

- Near-white background with deep burgundy headings and contact section.
- Bodoni Moda for display type and Manrope for navigation and practical information.
- Three uneven photo frames in the opening composition, followed by a staggered showcase.
- Quiet, expandable collection rows with honest empty package and price fields.
- Local identity and direct phone/text contact, without invented biographical details.
